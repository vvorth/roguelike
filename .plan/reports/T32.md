## The draw order — final, and binding (§23.5)

| outcome | draws |
|---|---|
| miss | **1** |
| blocked by a shield | **2** |
| hit, no poison | **2** |
| hit with poison | **3** |
| hit while `IMMUNE` | **1** |

`IMMUNE` returns before the damage roll, so it consumes no damage draw. Verified by counting
draws through a wrapper, not by inspection.

## The two measured traps, verified directly

**Resistance is on the raw roll.** With attacker STR 16 (+3), dagger 2–5, defender block 2 and
`RESISTANT`, the three possible placements give visibly different answers:

| placement | damage |
|---|---|
| **raw roll (the contract)** | **{2, 3}** |
| after the STR modifier | {1, 2} |
| after block | {1, 2, 3} |

Measured `{2, 3}`. The test asserting it spells out all three, so moving the step fails loudly.

**`IMMUNE` is not resurrected by the floor.** `damage == 0` with `hit is True`, and no poison.

## One correction to how the "shield never subtracts" check must be written

The obvious test — same seed, `shield_block=0` versus `shield_block=25`, assert identical
damage — **is wrong, and it fails**. The shield roll consumes a draw, so the two calls take
different values from the stream and legitimately produce different damage. It reported 253
mismatches on the first attempt.

What must actually hold is that the *distribution* is untouched: measured over 4000 seeds, the
damage value set is `{2, 3, 4, 5}` either way and the mean of unblocked hits differs by
`3.515` vs `3.516`. Both assertions are in the suite, with a comment recording why the per-seed
form is invalid — it is exactly the mistake a later reader would otherwise make.

Block rate tracks the shield: 10% → 9.0%, 18% → 17.3%, 25% → 24.4% of connecting attacks.

## Assumptions made
- `_resisted` handles only `RESISTANT`/`VULNERABLE`/`NORMAL`; `IMMUNE` is short-circuited by
  the caller, because it must bypass the `max(1, …)` floor and a roll multiplier cannot.
- A blocked attack returns before the resistance branch — a negated blow has no damage to
  resist, and this is what makes a block cost exactly two draws.
