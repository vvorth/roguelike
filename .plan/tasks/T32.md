# T32 — Resistance and the shield roll
Status: todo
Depends on: T29 (`items.py` complete)

## Goal
Teach the damage calculator two new things: a defender may resist the damage type, and a shield
may turn the blow entirely.

## Context
An interactive terminal roguelike, ADOM-ish, standard library only. Versions 1–5 are complete
(2615 tests). Version 6 gives the player an inventory, weapons with damage types, and shields.

**Your module is a pure calculator.** It takes two `Actor`s and some numbers and returns an
`AttackResult`. It does not know what an NPC is, emits no messages, removes nothing from the
world and ends nothing. That is what lets the same arithmetic serve both sides of every fight.

`combat.py` must NOT import `events`, `npc`, `level`, `world`, `game` or `curses`.

Three other workers are running in parallel on unrelated modules.

### The two traps here are both measured, not hypothetical

1. **Where resistance is applied is a 2× lever.** The same 50% resistance yields average damage
   from 1.25 to 2.50 purely from its position in the pipeline. §26.2 pins it to the **raw
   roll**, before the strength modifier and before block. Do not move it.
2. **`IMMUNE` must not be resurrected to 1 damage by the `max(1, …)` floor.** An immune
   defender takes **0**, and that is the one case where the floor does not apply.

Also inherited from v5 and still true — do not reintroduce either: there is **no attacker term
in to-hit**, and the STR modifier applies to **wielded weapons only**, never natural attacks.

Out of scope: armour, flat damage reduction of any kind (measured to saturate — §0.2 forbids
it), critical hits, damage-type resistance on the *player* beyond what the signature carries,
message generation, XP, death handling.

## Produce
`roguelike/combat.py`: `AttackResult.blocked`, two appended parameters on `resolve_attack`
(`resistance`, `shield_block`), and `ranged_block_chance` per §23.6. Plus `tests/test_combat.py`.

### Draw order — binding (§23.5)
1. to-hit (always) · 2. **shield roll** (only if hit and `shield_block > 0`) · 3. damage (only
if hit and not blocked) · 4. poison (only if damage was dealt and `poison_chance > 0`).

A blocked attack consumes exactly **two** draws, deals no damage and applies no poison — a
shield stops the venom with the fang.

## Constraints
- Python 3.10+, standard library only. Imports only `stats`, `items`, `status`.
- **No `Random` created or stored** — `rng` is passed in.
- Integer arithmetic only; `//` never `int(x/2)`.
- Pure: no mutation, no I/O, no module state.
- Both new parameters **appended with defaults**, so every v5 call site keeps working.

## Acceptance criteria
- Every v5 combat behaviour still holds: to-hit table, clamps, no attacker term, STR on wielded
  only, `max(1, …)` floor, miss semantics, poison never on a miss, purity, `ValueError` on
  `damage_min > damage_max`.
- **Resistance applied to the raw roll**: with a fixed rng, `RESISTANT` halves the *roll* and
  then STR and block apply. Assert the exact damage for a known roll under all four tiers, and
  add a test that fails if the multiplier is moved after the STR modifier.
- **`IMMUNE` gives `damage == 0` with `hit is True`** and no poison — assert explicitly that
  the floor did not raise it to 1.
- `VULNERABLE` doubles the roll; `NORMAL` changes nothing (identical to a v5 call).
- **Shield never subtracts.** When the block roll fails, damage is *identical* to the same call
  with `shield_block=0` and the same rng — assert it, because a flat-reduction implementation
  would pass a naive test.
- A successful block: `blocked is True`, `damage == 0`, `defender_hp` unchanged, `poisoned`
  `False` even at `poison_chance=100`.
- Block rate is roughly `shield_block` percent over many seeds (generous band, deterministic
  seeds).
- **Draw counts**: miss = 1 draw; blocked = 2; hit unblocked without poison = 2; hit with
  poison = 3. Count with a wrapper.
- `ranged_block_chance`: `clamp(base + (def_agi - atk_agi) * 2, 5, 75)`. Assert the coefficient
  is 2 (a 4-point gap moves it 8), the cap is 75 and the floor is 5, and that it never raises.
- Import purity via `ast`; no float literal.
