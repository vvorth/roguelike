# T34 — Chests and depth-scaled loot
Status: todo
Depends on: T29 (`items.py` complete)

## Goal
One new module: where a chest goes, how often, and what is in it.

## Context
An interactive terminal roguelike, ADOM-ish, standard library only. Versions 1–5 are complete
(2615 tests). Version 6 adds items, and chests are the only source of them — no monster drops,
no shops, no floor litter.

Three other workers are running in parallel on unrelated modules.

**Chests are not terrain, and this is the load-bearing constraint.** `Level` is a frozen,
generated map; an opened chest changes and terrain cannot. This is exactly the problem doors
had, solved the same way: mutable state lives *outside* `Level` (CONTRACT-v2 §0.6, the
`open_doors` precedent). **There is no `Tile.CHEST` and `generator.py` is frozen.** Your module
produces `Chest` values; `game.py` stores them on `LevelState` next to `npcs`.

Out of scope: opening a chest, the inventory, rendering, monster drops, shops, keys or locks,
traps, identification. Extra surface is a defect.

## Produce
`roguelike/loot.py` (new) and `tests/test_loot.py` (new), per §27:
`Chest`, `CHEST_CHANCE`, `CHEST_CHANCE_DEEP`, `DEEP_FROM`, `CHEST_SAFE_RADIUS`,
`chest_chance(depth)`, `grade_weights(depth)`, `place_chest(rng, level, depth)`.

### The depth table — binding (§27.1), assert every row literally
| depth | crude | standard | fine |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | 80% | 19% | **1%** |
| 4–6 | 55% | 40% | **5%** |
| 7–9 | 30% | 60% | **10%** |
| 10+ | 15% | 70% | **15%** |

A table, not a curve — someone will want to tune one row without re-deriving a formula.

### Placement (§27.2)
At most one chest per level, at `chest_chance(depth)` percent. Only on a cell passable with no
door open, at least `CHEST_SAFE_RADIUS` (8, Chebyshev) from `level.player_start`. Contents:
**1–3 items**, each rolled independently against `grade_weights(depth)`.

## Constraints
- Python 3.10+, standard library only. Imports **only** `roguelike.items` and
  `roguelike.level`. Must not import `game`, `npc`, `combat`, `render`, or `curses`.
- **No `Random` created or stored** — `rng` is passed in, exactly as `spawn_npcs` does.
- Frozen dataclasses, pure functions, integer arithmetic, no floats.
- Deterministic: the same seed, level and depth give an identical chest.
- **Termination must be structural, not a retry budget** — choose from the list of legal cells
  and filter, do not guess and re-roll. On a map with no legal cell, return `None`; never hang,
  never relax the radius.

## Acceptance criteria
- `chest_chance`: 12 for depths 1–9, 8 from 10 up; `DEEP_FROM == 10`; never raises for any
  depth ≥ 1.
- **Every row of §27.1 asserted literally**, and each row's three weights sum to 100.
- The `fine` weight is monotonically non-decreasing with depth and is **1% at depth 1** — the
  "extremely low chance of a higher grade" requirement, asserted as such.
- `place_chest` over **many seeds and several generated levels**: the position is always
  passable, always ≥ 8 Chebyshev from `player_start`, contents are 1–3 items, and every item is
  drawn from `items.py`'s constants.
- Grade distribution over many seeds is roughly `grade_weights(depth)` at depths 1, 5 and 12 —
  generous bands so it is not flaky.
- **Determinism**: same seed → identical chest; and identical across two `PYTHONHASHSEED`
  values in a `subprocess`.
- On a level with no legal cell (tiny, or every cell inside the safe radius) `place_chest`
  returns `None`, does not raise, and does not hang — with a strict wall-clock guard.
- `Chest` is frozen and defaults `opened=False`.
- Import purity via `ast`, asserting explicitly that `game`, `npc`, `combat`, `render` and
  `curses` are absent. No float literal.
- Tests pass with no TTY. No `conftest.py`.
