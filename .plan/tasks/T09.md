# T09 — Generator: door placement constraint
Status: todo
Depends on: none

## Goal
Make every generated door sit properly embedded in a wall run, by routing corridors into rooms
perpendicular to the wall they pierce.

## Context
This is an interactive terminal roguelike engine with ASCII graphics, similar in feel to ADOM.
Version 1 is complete and passing 912 tests. You are fixing a real defect in the dungeon
generator, measured on the current build.

The engine is built by independent workers who never communicate; a frozen contract defines
every seam. Two other workers are running in parallel on unrelated files. Your task touches no
file they touch and is fully independent of the rest of the v2 work.

### The defect, measured

Scanned **400 seeds / 8318 doors** on the current build:

- **1090 doors (13.1%)** are not embedded in a wall run — they have open floor on one side and
  wall on the other, in both axes.
- **2525 doors** are orthogonally adjacent to another door.

Every violating door sits at a **corner junction**:

```
seed 0, bad door at (58,16)        seed 2, bad door at (32,14)
   .........                          ####.....
   ####+....   ← door                 +...+....   ← door with floor on BOTH sides
   #########                          #####....
```

### Root cause

`_carve_corridor` turns a corridor cell into a door whenever `_is_wall_piercing` finds it on a
room perimeter and stepping into that room's floor. That test never looks at the cells
*flanking* the door along the wall. Corridors are centre-to-centre L-shaped doglegs, so when an
elbow lands near a room corner the path clips the perimeter diagonally and produces a dangling
door.

### The mandated fix

**Reroute, not demote.** Corridors must approach a room's wall **perpendicularly**, entering
through a perimeter cell that is embedded in a wall run.

A one-line alternative was measured and **rejected**: refusing to mark bad piercings as doors
and carving `FLOOR` instead satisfies the letter of the constraint but leaves **6.3% of rooms
(271/4280) with no door at all**, entered through an unmarked corner gap. That trades a
malformed door for a missing one. Do not do it.

### What must not regress

The generator carries twelve guarantees. Two are easy to break with a routing change and fail
**silently**:

- **G8 full connectivity** — a 4-directional flood fill from `player_start` must reach every
  walkable tile. Reroute changes corridor shape; this is the guard.
- **G1/G2 determinism** — one local `random.Random(seed)`, no global `random`. Changing the
  *order* of RNG draws changes every level, which is allowed; making the result irreproducible
  is not.

Out of scope, explicitly: monsters, combat, items, inventory, save-load, multiple levels,
stairs, sound, colour. No spawn points, no room themes, no corridor decoration. Do not touch
visibility or fog of war — other workers own that.

Coordinates are `(x, y)`, origin top-left, `y` grows **down**, grid storage `grid[y][x]`.
`Room` describes the **floor rectangle only**; its walls are the 1-cell ring outside it.

### The new door guarantees
- **G9a** Every `Tile.DOOR` lies on some room's `on_perimeter`.
- **G9b** Embedded in a wall run: either `(x-1, y)` **and** `(x+1, y)` are both `Tile.WALL`, or
  `(x, y-1)` **and** `(x, y+1)` are both `Tile.WALL`.
- **G9c** The perpendicular axis is passage: if G9b matched vertically, `(x-1, y)` and
  `(x+1, y)` are both non-`WALL`; if horizontally, `(x, y-1)` and `(x, y+1)` are both
  non-`WALL`.
- **G9d** No door is orthogonally adjacent to another door.
- **G4a** Every room has at least one `Tile.DOOR` on its perimeter, except a single-room level,
  which has none.

Out-of-bounds neighbours count as `Tile.WALL` for G9b/G9c.

## Constraints
- Python 3.10+, **standard library only**. `from __future__ import annotations` stays.
- Imports from the project: **only** `roguelike.tiles` and `roguelike.level` (unchanged).
  No `curses`.
- **Determinism is the hardest constraint.** One local `rng = random.Random(seed)`. Module-level
  `random.randint` / `random.choice` / `random.shuffle` / `random.random` remain **forbidden**,
  as does any dependence on `time`, `os.urandom`, `id()`, `hash()`, or set-iteration order.
- **Do not change the public signature, the four constants, or the error behaviour** in
  CONTRACT §3.1 (`TypeError` for a non-int or bool seed, `ValueError` for `max_rooms < 1` or
  dimensions below `MIN_ROOM_SIZE + 2`).
- G1–G8 and G10–G12 must continue to hold. Extend `_assert_guarantees` to cover G9a–d and G4a
  so the generator never hands back a level that violates them.
- Changing the number or order of RNG draws is **allowed** — levels for a given seed may differ
  from v1. They must simply be reproducible. Do not pin any test to a v1 grid snapshot.
- Placing fewer rooms than `max_rooms` remains correct. Placing zero remains a bug (G4).
- Keep it readable. A corridor router nobody can follow is a liability.
- Tests must not initialise curses and must pass with no TTY. No `conftest.py`.
- The existing 381 tests in `tests/test_generator.py` must still pass. Where a test asserted the
  old (loose) door rule, tighten it rather than delete it.

## Acceptance criteria
- **G9b/G9c hold for every door over at least 200 seeds** — zero violations. This is the
  headline criterion; the current build fails it on 13.1% of doors.
- **G9d holds** — no door orthogonally adjacent to another, over at least 200 seeds.
- **G9a holds** — every door on some room's perimeter; no door inside any room's floor rect.
- **G4a holds** — every room has at least one door, over at least 200 seeds, at the default
  size and at `(40, 15)`. A single-room level has zero doors and that is accepted.
- **G8 connectivity holds** — flood fill from `player_start` reaches every walkable tile, over
  at least 30 seeds and at three map sizes. Implement the flood fill in the test independently.
- **G1 determinism** — two calls with identical arguments produce equal `Level`s including
  `rooms` order; tested over at least five seeds.
- **G2 cross-process determinism** — a fixed seed's grid digest recomputed in a `subprocess`
  under at least two different `PYTHONHASHSEED` values matches the in-process value.
- Source scan: `random.Random(` present; `random.randint(`, `random.choice(`, `random.shuffle(`,
  `random.random(`, `random.randrange(`, `random.seed(` absent at module level.
- G3 (border all `WALL`), G5 (rooms disjoint at `margin=1`), G6 (rooms in bounds with margin),
  G7 (room floors carved), G10 (`player_start` in a room and walkable), G11, G12 all still hold
  over several seeds and sizes.
- `generate_level(0, MIN_ROOM_SIZE + 2, MIN_ROOM_SIZE + 2)` still returns a valid single-room
  level satisfying G3, G4, G8, G10.
- Error cases unchanged: `TypeError` for `"abc"`, `1.0`, `True`; `ValueError` for
  `max_rooms=0`, `width=MIN_ROOM_SIZE + 1`, `height=MIN_ROOM_SIZE + 1`.
- No `FLOOR` or `DOOR` cell on the border.
- A test that reproduces the **specific historical failures** — assert that the cells at
  `(58, 16)` for seed 0, `(66, 6)` for seed 1, `(32, 14)` for seed 2 and `(20, 18)` for seed 3
  either are not doors or now satisfy G9b/G9c. (The map for these seeds will change; the point
  is that the corner-junction shape no longer produces a door anywhere.)
