# T36 — Wiring: inventory, commands, chests
Status: todo
Depends on: T29–T35 (all complete and verified)

## Goal
Connect the whole of v6 to the turn loop. You are the last worker.

## Context
An interactive terminal roguelike, ADOM-ish, standard library only. Versions 1–5 are complete;
the suite stood at **2615 passing** when v6 began. Seven v6 modules are finished and verified —
items and inventory, resistance and shield rolls, species resistances, chests and loot, the
chest glyph, the regeneration effect, the keys and the wording. **None of it is connected to
anything.** You connect it.

**`game.py` is the only module allowed to import this widely, and the only one that holds game
rules.** `step` and `advance` are **pure** — same state in, same state out, no terminal, no
clock, no I/O, no randomness that is not derived from the state. `run` owns the keyboard and
the clock and holds no rules.

### v6's one breaking change

`Player.melee` and `Player.ranged` move into `Player.inventory`. Every read of `player.melee`
becomes `player.inventory.melee`. It is deliberate — keeping equipment on `Player` *and* adding
a shield slot would put it in two places — and you own every call site. The full suite is the
check.

### Read the seven reports before writing code

`.plan/reports/T29.md` … `T35.md` state the exact surfaces. Four things in particular:

- **`tick_effects` now returns THREE values**: `(effects, damage, healing)`. Not a signed net —
  a net of zero cannot tell "nothing happened" from "2 damage and 2 healing", and you word
  those differently.
- **`resolve_attack` gains `resistance` and `shield_block`**, both appended. You decide which
  chance to pass: a shield's plain `block_chance` for melee, and
  `combat.ranged_block_chance(...)` for a missile. `resolve_attack` rolls what it is given and
  cannot tell the difference.
- **`npc.resistance_of(species, damage_type) -> Resistance`** — two positional arguments.
- **The renderer call is now**
  `render_to_cells(level, player_pos, visible, explored, open_doors, chrome, npcs, target,
  projectile, chests)` with `chests` a `frozenset[Coord]`.

### Four things that are easy to get wrong

1. **A rejected move consumes no turn, so the world must not tick.** v1's headline rule,
   surviving five increments.
2. **`equip` raises on a `Consumable`** (T29's decision). Dispatch "use" *before* calling it.
3. **Monsters are placed after chests** (CONTRACT-v6 §27.4) — an amendment issued because
   `loot.py` cannot see monsters. A monster must not start on the level's only chest.
4. **Bare-handed is representable**: a `None` melee slot attacks for 1–2 BLUNT with
   `strength_applies=True`; a `None` ranged slot refuses to fire.

Out of scope: food, hunger, armour, weight, stacking, identification, shops, monster drops,
ammunition, `Tile.CHEST`.

## Constraints
- Python 3.10+, standard library only. `from __future__ import annotations`.
- `step` and `advance` stay **pure**: no mutation, no I/O, no clock, no `time.sleep`, no
  module-level `random`. All randomness derives from `roll_seed`.
- `run` owns the clock; `timeout` stays the only pacing mechanism.
- Every dataclass frozen; `state.saved` rebuilt, never mutated.
- New `GameState` fields **appended with defaults**, in the order §7 v6 gives.
- **If you believe another module has a bug, report it — do not fix it.**

## Acceptance criteria
- Every v1–v5 behaviour survives: a rejected move costs no turn **and does not tick the world**;
  bump-to-attack on hostiles; swap with non-hostiles; the walk/target/help/look/rest sub-modes;
  auto-navigation refused and interrupted while a hostile is in view; levelling; death.
- `PICK_UP` on a chest takes one item and **costs a turn**; on an empty cell reports and costs
  none; with a full pack reports `PACK_FULL` and costs none.
- `INVENTORY` opens the screen and **costs no turn, ever** — like help and look. Browsing costs
  none; equipping and drinking cost one each.
- Equipping a weapon changes which damage the next attack does; equipping a shield makes blows
  blockable — assert both through a real attack, not just the slot.
- **Resistance reaches combat**: a PIERCE weapon against the cave snake does measurably less
  than a BLUNT one; the giant bat takes more from BLUNT. Assert via the real `step`.
- **Bare-handed** works: 1–2 BLUNT melee; `f` with no ranged weapon refuses and costs no turn.
- Chests are placed at generation, persist on `LevelState` across a stairs round trip, and
  **no monster starts on a chest**.
- A bandage applies a regeneration effect that heals over turns; a potion heals instantly. Both
  cost a turn.
- **Purity and determinism**: the same seed and key sequence give an identical final state,
  across two `PYTHONHASHSEED` values in a `subprocess`.
- No `Random` stored on any state; `game.py` contains no clock read.
